NBA TRADE CBA RULES REFERENCE
2026-27 SYSTEM VALUES
- Salary Cap: $164.961 million
- Luxury Tax: $200.428 million
- First Apron: $209.015 million
- Second Apron: $221.686 million
1. TEAM USING CAP SPACE
A team below the salary cap can acquire salary up to: Cap room + $250,000
2. STANDARD TRADED PLAYER EXCEPTION
For one outgoing player, a team can generally acquire: 100% of outgoing salary + $250,000
If the team is above the First Apron after the transaction, the $250,000 cushion is not available.
A non-simultaneous Traded Player Exception generally expires one year after the original trade.
3. AGGREGATED SALARY MATCHING
Teams may normally combine the salaries of multiple outgoing players.
Using the Aggregated Standard TPE is restricted by the Second Apron.
A team cannot use aggregation if doing so would leave its Apron Team Salary above the Second Apron.
4. EXPANDED TRADED PLAYER EXCEPTION
Teams sufficiently below the First Apron may use expanded salary matching.
Maximum incoming salary is generally the maximum permitted under the expanded
salary-matching tiers.
For 2026-27, approximately:
- Outgoing <= $8.846M: incoming <= 200% of outgoing + $250K
- Outgoing between about $8.846M and $35.383M: incoming <= outgoing + about $9.096M
- Outgoing >= about $35.383M: incoming <= 125% of outgoing + $250K
Using the Expanded TPE hard-caps the team at the First Apron.
5. FIRST APRON RESTRICTIONSCertain transactions hard-cap a team at the First Apron, including:
- Acquiring a player via sign-and-trade
- Using the Expanded Traded Player Exception
- Certain uses of pre-existing trade exceptions and other first-apron-triggering mechanisms
Validation:
If a transaction triggers the First Apron hard cap and post-trade Apron Team Salary exceeds the
First Apron, the trade is illegal.
6. SECOND APRON RESTRICTIONS
If a transaction would leave a team above the Second Apron, major restrictions apply.
Important examples:
- Cannot aggregate multiple outgoing salaries in the prohibited manner
- Cannot send cash in a trade
- Cannot use certain trade exceptions generated from sign-and-trade transactions
- Cannot use mechanisms already restricted at the First Apron
- Cannot use the additional $250,000 matching cushion once above the First Apron
7. FREE-AGENT SIGNING TRADE RESTRICTION
A player signing a standard NBA contract as a free agent generally cannot be traded until the
later of:
- Three months after signing
- December 15 of that salary-cap year
8. JANUARY 15 RESTRICTION
Certain players re-signing with their prior team cannot be traded until the later of:
- Three months after signing
- January 15
This generally applies when:
- The player re-signs with his prior team
- Bird or Early Bird rights are used
- The team is above the cap after signing
- First-year salary is more than 120% of the previous salary
- The contract is not simply a minimum-salary deal
9. NEWLY SIGNED DRAFT PICKS
A Draft Rookie who signs his NBA contract generally cannot be traded for 30 days after signing.Unsigned draft rights may be traded.
10. TWO-WAY PLAYERS
Two-Way players generally have a 30-day trade restriction after signing.
Trading a Two-Way player does not create a Traded Player Exception.
11. RECENTLY ACQUIRED PLAYER AGGREGATION RESTRICTION
A recently acquired player may be tradable individually but may not be eligible to have his
salary aggregated with another player's salary for two months after acquisition in applicable
situations.
12. ONE-YEAR BIRD-RIGHTS TRADE VETO
A player on certain one-year contracts who would obtain Bird or Early Bird rights at the end of
the deal may have the right to veto a trade unless that right was waived.
13. CONTRACTUAL NO-TRADE CLAUSES
Some players have explicit no-trade clauses.
A legal salary match does not override contractual trade-consent rights.
14. MATCHED RESTRICTED-FREE-AGENT OFFER SHEETS
When a team matches an RFA offer sheet:
- The player generally cannot be traded without consent for one year
- The player generally cannot be traded to the team that originally submitted the offer sheet for one year
15. DESIGNATED VETERAN CONTRACTS / EXTENSIONS
A player who signs a qualifying Designated Veteran contract or extension generally cannot be
traded for one year from the signing date.
16. RECENTLY EXTENDED OR RENEGOTIATED PLAYERS
Certain extensions create a six-month trade restriction.
Renegotiated contracts may also create a six-month restriction.
17. ROOKIE EXTENSION POISON PILL
A player who signs a Rookie Scale Extension and is traded before the extension begins may have
different outgoing and incoming salary values for trade matching.The acquiring team's trade calculation may use an average salary across the current season and
extension seasons rather than simply the current rookie salary.
18. SIGN-AND-TRADE REQUIREMENTS
A sign-and-trade generally requires:
- Player is the signing team's own free agent
- Player finished the prior season on that team's roster
- Signing occurs before the regular season begins
- Contract lasts at least three seasons excluding an option year
- Contract lasts no more than four seasons including an option year
- First season is fully guaranteed
Receiving a sign-and-traded player hard-caps the receiving team at the First Apron.
19. SIGN-AND-TRADE BASE YEAR COMPENSATION
Certain Bird or Early Bird sign-and-trades with large raises use special outgoing salary
treatment.
For qualifying transactions, the signing team may count the outgoing player at the greater of:
- Previous salary
- 50% of the new first-year salary
The acquiring team counts the player's full new salary.
20. EXPIRING CONTRACTS AFTER THE TRADE DEADLINE
After the trade deadline, players whose contracts expire that season, or could expire because of
an option or ETO, generally cannot be traded in the post-season trade window before that
contract ends.
21. REACQUIRING A TRADED-AND-WAIVED PLAYER
A team generally cannot trade a player away, have him waived, and immediately reacquire him.
The original team generally must wait until the earlier of:
- One year after the trade
- July 1 following the final season of the player's contract
22. NON-GUARANTEED CONTRACTS
Do not always use headline salary as the player's outgoing trade value.
Trade salary may depend on:- Guaranteed salary
- Earned salary
- Guarantee dates
- Applicable CBA treatment at the date of the trade
23. TRADE KICKERS / TRADE BONUSES
A trade may trigger a contractual trade bonus.
The bonus may alter the player's cap or trade salary.
Trade bonuses cannot push a player above the applicable maximum salary.
Players may waive some or all of certain trade bonuses when permitted.
24. CASH CONSIDERATIONS
Teams may send or receive cash in trades subject to annual aggregate limits.
For 2026-27, the annual amount is approximately 5.15% of the salary cap, or about $8.495
million.
Cash sent and cash received are tracked separately.
A team cannot send cash in a trade if prohibited by Second Apron rules.
25. FUTURE DRAFT-PICK HORIZON
Teams generally cannot trade draft picks more than seven future drafts into the future.
26. STEPIEN RULE
A team cannot make a trade that could leave it without a first-round pick in two consecutive
future drafts.
The rule concerns whether the team possesses a first-round selection, not necessarily its own
original pick.
27. PROTECTED PICKS AND THE STEPIEN RULE
Pick protections must be modeled across every possible conveyance year.
If a protected first-round pick can roll into later seasons, every possible conveyance scenario
must satisfy the Stepien Rule.The engine should model obligation trees rather than only a simple owned/not-owned flag.
28. PICK SWAPS
Pick swaps generally do not violate the Stepien Rule by themselves because the team still
receives a first-round pick.
This is why major trades often alternate outright first-round picks with swaps.
29. SECOND APRON FROZEN FIRST-ROUND PICK
If a team's Apron Team Salary is above the Second Apron at the relevant season-end
measurement point, its first-round pick seven drafts into the future can become frozen and
untradeable.
If the team remains above the Second Apron often enough in the following years, the frozen pick
may be moved to the end of the first round.
30. TRADE DEADLINE
No normal player trades may be completed after the NBA trade deadline until the applicable
post-season trading window opens.
The exact deadline should be stored as a season-level variable rather than hardcoded.
31. MULTI-TEAM TRADE VALIDATION
For three-team or larger transactions, do not validate only total incoming and outgoing salary
across the whole transaction.
Validate each participating team independently.
Each team may rely on a different mechanism, such as:
- Cap room
- Standard TPE
- Aggregated Standard TPE
- Expanded TPE
- Existing TPE
